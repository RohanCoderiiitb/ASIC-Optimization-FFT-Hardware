Clock period: 10.0 ns
Energy/FFT = Power(mW) * ExecCycles * ClockPeriod(ns) / 1000, ExecCycles from ../sim/perf/fp16_sqnr_results.txt
Power is measured from a SAIF file converted (vcd_to_saif.py) from a real RTL-simulation VCD -- the same 11 SQNR signals, same testbench timing -- and annotated by OpenSTA's `read_saif` onto a hierarchy-preserved netlist (ActivitySrc=saif_measured) when at least 20 nets match (AnnotatedSignals=annotated/total). There is NO flat-activity fallback: if SAIF generation fails or coverage is below that floor, the row is ActivitySrc=SAIF_ANNOTATION_FAILED / SYNTH_FAILED and Status=FAILED, with Power/Energy-FFT reported as N/A rather than a misleading number -- see run_yosys()/generate_activity_saif() for why flattening breaks name matching.
