BEST DESIGN PER FFT SIZE (from the NSGA-II results)
==============================================================================

Rule      : balanced: equal-weight distance to the ideal point over energy, area, critical path and SQNR (lowest wins)
Pool      : Pareto-optimal designs that meet timing (slack >= 0 ns), SQNR >= 15 dB; mixed FP4+FP8 designs preferred
Area      : equal for every design (fixed floorplan), so it cannot separate them.
